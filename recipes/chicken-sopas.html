Chicken Sopas

A warm and creamy Filipino macaroni soup!

Description

Chicken Sopas is a comforting Filipino soup made with elbow macaroni, shredded chicken, vegetables, milk, and broth. It is commonly enjoyed during rainy days and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups cooked and shredded chicken
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 cup evaporated milk
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat cooking oil in a pot.
  2. Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  3. Add the shredded chicken and stir.
  4. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a boil.
  5. Add the macaroni and cook until tender.
  6. Add the carrots and cabbage.
  7. Pour in the evaporated milk and stir gently.
  8. Season with salt and pepper.
  9. Serve warm.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time Cooking Time Servings
15 minutes 25 minutes 4 people
